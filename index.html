Paper → Diagrams

Upload one research paper. The embedded Python scripts read it, draw 5 black-and-white diagrams from its own content, rate how much each one can be trusted, and tell you which section each belongs in — then optionally a second pass adds 3 advanced plates. Everything runs inside your browser; the paper is never uploaded anywhere.
